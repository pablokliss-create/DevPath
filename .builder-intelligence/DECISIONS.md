# Durable Decisions

- `curso-programacao-nu` is legacy and rejected; never confuse it with DevPath.
- Pedagogy optimizes for mental models, diagnosis, transfer, and retention.
- Research curriculum continuously against strong free/open resources, university material, communities, Git history, issues, PRs, and changelogs.
- No tool or framework is a permanent default.
- Architecture decisions should explicitly consider lock-in, exit cost, portability, longevity, and rework risk.
- Materially uncertain structural choices should be benchmarked or prototyped before consolidation.
